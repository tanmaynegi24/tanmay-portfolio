Real estate private equity mandate
Four months modelling asset-level returns and stress-testing investment cases.
Read

Problem

Real estate returns depend on occupancy, rental growth and exit yield, and small changes in any of them move the result.

What I did

Modelled returns asset by asset and ran cases on the assumptions that moved the outcome most, so the downside was visible.

What it showed

The most useful output of scenario work was finding the one assumption each case depended on.

Problem

ESG exposure sits outside the financial statements but can affect valuation at exit.

What I did

Benchmarked each portfolio company against 10+ peers in its own sector on a consistent set of risk factors, and flagged the regulatory exposure that came up.

What it showed
