Screening 300+ companies across six sectors
Building the coverage universe a private equity fund sources deals from.

Problem

Deal teams needed a current view of who was operating in their target markets, at what scale, and with what financial profile.

What I did

Profiled 300+ companies to a consistent template covering business model, revenue scale, ownership, growth and margins, and recent corporate activity. Sourced through FactSet and Capital IQ and distilled into 7+ industry one-pagers that gave deal teams an entry point into unfamiliar markets.

What it showed

Screening quality depends on consistency rather than volume. A template that asks the same questions of every company is what makes an outlier visible.

Read

Problem

Every investment question reduces to a number the investment committee is willing to defend.

What I did

Built DCF models for acquisition targets and portfolio companies to establish intrinsic value, then held that against what the market was paying by benchmarking 30+ peers across three recurring comps cycles.

What it showed

The peer set carries most of the argument. Much of the disagreement in a valuation is settled before a cash flow is projected.

Two bolt-on acquisitions
Screening, transaction comparables and precedent review for a portfolio company.
Read

Problem

A portfolio company needed to grow through acquisition. Which targets fit, and at what price.

What I did

Screened the target universe against strategic fit criteria, then built transaction comparables and reviewed precedent deals to establish the multiple range the market had cleared for similar assets.

What it showed

Precedent transactions price a moment as much as an asset. Deals struck under different rates need discounting as evidence, not just adjusting as arithmetic.

Real estate private equity mandate
Four months modelling asset-level returns and stress-testing investment cases.
Read

Problem

Real estate returns rest on assumptions that hold until they do not: occupancy, rental growth, exit yield.

What I did

Modelled returns at the individual asset level and ran cases across the assumptions that moved the outcome most, so the downside was explicit.

What it showed

Scenario work earns its keep when it identifies the single assumption the investment case is resting on.

ESG risk benchmarking
Four portfolio companies benchmarked against 10+ sector peers each.
Read

Problem

ESG exposure sits outside the financial statements but shows up in valuation at exit.

What I did

Benchmarked each portfolio company against 10+ peers in its own sector on a consistent set of risk factors, and flagged the regulatory exposure that surfaced.

What it showed

An absolute ESG score says little. The same profile is a liability in one sector and unremarkable in another.

India space technology sector thesis
A 15-slide whitepaper with an accompanying global sector map. Every figure sourced.

Problem

India's space sector opened to private capital faster than a coherent investment view of it formed.

What I did

Mapped the sector into its operating layers, launch, satellite manufacturing, ground infrastructure, and the data and analytics layer above them, then built the whitepaper as fourteen sections argued from sourced evidence.

My contribution

What it showed

Value is migrating from hardware toward the data, analytics and in-orbit AI layer. Launch is becoming infrastructure, while the margin accumulates with whoever owns what the hardware produces.

A 15-slide India whitepaper and an 8-slide global sector map, produced with Praxel and presented to faculty and cohort.
